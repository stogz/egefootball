    Rarity & Odds

    How cards are graded and how often each pulls

    The number in a card's corner is its fantasy score, half PPR, the same scoring a game pays credits on. A play is scored as the one play. Rarity is graded from that score against the position, so a tight end's big night is as rare as a quarterback's. A play is graded as if every play were carried or caught, so a 60 yard touchdown pass grades with a 60 yard touchdown catch, and go-ahead, game-tying and game-winning plays grade a little higher.

    Each card in a pack rolls its rarity first, at the chance below, then is drawn from every card of that rarity that is out.

    Weeks

    The season file holds every result of the year, and nobody sees any of it until you put a week out. Publishing shows the scores and the stat lines, moves the records, pays the game credits and posts the week to Discord — one button. Pulling a week back is one click too, as often as you like.

    Correcting a stat line after its week is out is fine: Pay credits works out what everybody is owed again and pays the difference. It never pays the same game twice, and it never takes credits back from a number that went down — adjust a balance by hand for that.

    End of Season

    Four steps, in this order. Nothing is cleared until the file that replaces it is in the repository, so a step out of order costs nobody their season.

    1. 1

      Lock the ratings

      Downloads data/ratings.js with every rating point and every workout bought this season folded into the base numbers. Commit it, and the improvements are part of the site rather than part of a database. It can be downloaded again at any point; nothing is counted twice.

      Player Attributes moved Points Overall
    2. 2

      Log the season

      Downloads data/logs/season-.js: the stats posted in every game, which booster was riding on which game, and every credit earned and spent. Commit it next to the ratings.

    3. 3

      Clear the shop rows

      Wipes everybody's rating points and offseason workouts, which the ratings file now carries instead. Performance boosters nobody has used stay where they are, and Intel lapses on its own. Do this only once both files above are committed and live.

    4. 4

      Roll the season over

      Downloads data/season.js pointing at the next season. Commit it and everybody is paid their offseason allowance the next time they open the site.
